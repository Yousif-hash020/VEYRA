About This Escape

Perched amidst ancient pine ridge forests at 2,200 meters altitude in Nathia Gali, this handcrafted timber cabin redefines mountain tranquility. Designed for discerning travelers, the retreat combines authentic cedar-wood architecture with modern Scandinavian minimalist luxury.

Savor morning espresso on the cantilevered wooden deck overlooking mist-shrouded Himalayan valleys, gather around the slate wood-burning fireplace in the double-height ceiling living room, or unwind under starlit nights in total privacy.
